Orks 11th Edition

Army Rules

Waaagh!

  • Passive: Friendly ORKS units with this ability can re-roll Advance rolls.
  • War Cry (Once per battle): At the start of the Command phase, you can activate this ability. Friendly ORKS units with Waaagh! become Riled up until the end of the next turn.

While Riled Up:

  • Unit gains a 5+ Invulnerable Save (InSv).
  • Ranged attacks gain [Assault].
  • Advancing does not prevent the unit from being eligible to declare a charge.

Da Boss

At the start of the battle round, if a model with this ability is your Warlord, you gain 1 CP.

Unstable Energies

ORKS PSYKER units have a designated Psyker Level. In a single battle round, a Psyker can use psychic abilities whose combined levels do not exceed the unit's Psyker Level.

Special Move Types

  • Pulse Jet Move: Max distance is D6 + 18". Ignores vertical distance, moves through models/terrain. After moving, the unit must be unengaged, cannot charge or start an action, and attacks targeting it suffer -1 to hit until your next turn.
  • Assault Disembark Move: Set up within 3". Eligible only if embarked on a Transport that hasn't advanced/fallen back this phase (and unit hasn't embarked this phase).

Detachment: Green Tide

Detachment Rule: Mob-handed Brutality

  • Friendly BOYZ units' melee attacks gain [Sustained Hits 1].
  • If a friendly ORKS INFANTRY unit makes a charge move this turn, its melee attacks gain [Lethal Hits] against non-Monster/Vehicle targets.

Detachment: Runt Swarm

Detachment Rule: Sneaky Little Gitz

  • GRETCHIN units have Battleline.
  • GRETCHIN units can perform actions even if they used advance or fall-back moves

Runt Swarm Stratagems

Grot Shields1CP
Opponent's Shooting Phase

When the enemy shoots at an Infantry unit within 3" of a Gretchin mob, the grots intercept. The Gretchin unit takes 1 Mortal Wound for each successful hit, and the attack sequence ends immediately.

Scarper!1CP
Opponent's Movement Phase

If an enemy unit ends its movement within 8" of a Gretchin unit, the Gretchin can make a reactive move of D6" (or a flat 6" if a Character is attached).

Infested War Zone2CP
Any Phase

When a Gretchin squad is destroyed, you can immediately respawn the exact same mob in Strategic Reserves.

Detachment: Shoota Boyz

Detachment Rule: Dakka! Dakka! Dakka!

  • Friendly ORKS INFANTRY units' ranged attacks gain [Assault].
  • While the unit is Riled up, their ranged attacks receive +3" Range.
